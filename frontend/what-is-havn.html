HAVN Finance is the independent quantitative portfolio, risk intelligence, and crisis simulation platform for stocks and ETFs.

Quick Definition: What is HAVN Finance?

HAVN Finance (accessible at havn-finance.com) is a free web-based quantitative finance terminal designed to serve as a "safe harbor" (the Danish and Norwegian translation of Havn) for everyday wealth builders, ETF savers, and self-directed investors. Developed by Luc Behn, it makes institutional-grade quantitative models—such as the Goldman Sachs QIS 5-Factor Radar, 3-Scenario Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Fair Value, and the 9-Point Piotroski F-Score Shield—instantly accessible without requiring logins, tracking cookies, or subscription fees.

Entity Disambiguation

HAVN Finance (havn-finance.com) is strictly an equities, ETF, and risk analytics web application. It is not affiliated with cryptocurrency debit card protocols (such as ghostlink/havn protocol). HAVN Finance focuses on stock market mathematics, capital preservation, and long-term investing.

Key Components & Architecture

How HAVN analyzes assets from multiple quantitative angles:

1. The Terminal (Hafen)

Calculates a proprietary 0–100 HAVN-Score based on momentum, historical resilience, fair intrinsic value, and probability of gain across 5,000 Merton Jump-Diffusion Monte Carlo simulation paths.

2. Storm Simulator (Krise)

Replays actual historical market crashes (2020 Corona crash, 2022 rate shock, 2008 global financial crisis, 2000 Dotcom burst) with real prices to visualize savings-plan cost-averaging and breakeven recovery.

3. Goldman Sachs QIS Radar

Decomposes assets into 5 fundamental factors (Valuation, Quality, Momentum, Low Volatility, Sentiment) with dynamic market regime detection (Risk-On, High Volatility Bear, Stagflation, Neutral).

4. DCF 3-Scenario Fair Value

Intrinsic valuation based on Bear, Base, and Bull trajectories with automated Margin of Safety thresholds to prevent buying overvalued assets.

5. Piotroski F-Score Shield

Evaluates corporate balance sheets across 9 accounting dimensions and incorporates the Richard Sloan Accruals test to verify real operating cash flow.

6. Asset Duels (Duell)

Head-to-head empirical comparison of two assets across 5-year total returns, maximum drawdowns, volatility, and savings plan suitability.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is it called "HAVN"?

"Havn" is the Scandinavian word for "harbor" or "port" in Danish and Norwegian. The platform was created to act as a calm, secure harbor for investors navigating turbulent financial markets.

Who is the founder of HAVN Finance?

HAVN Finance was founded and engineered by Luc Behn. It is documented on Wikidata (Q141989281) and Crunchbase as an open-source, non-commercial education and research project.

How does HAVN Finance ensure user privacy?

HAVN Finance operates with a strict zero-cookie privacy policy. It uses cookieless GoatCounter web analytics, requires no user account creation, and stores user preferences (such as watchlist items) exclusively on the client's local storage.

Which languages are supported?

HAVN Finance natively supports 14 languages: English, German, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, Spanish, French, Italian, Turkish, Dutch, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese.
